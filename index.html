04. Personal Reflection on Employability Skills

This module has centered around employability skills that will aid me in my future role. At the start of the module, I felt that I needed to develop skills in teamwork, problem-solving, leadership, communication, and analytical thinking. Using the FreshCart Online scenario, I have been able to see an application to the issues that can arise in the workplace.

Communication and Teamwork

My knowledge of communication has been developed through looking at sharing issues in relation to information between office and warehouse staff at FreshCart. I have identified that clarity of communication, feedback, active listening, and clarification are all important to prevent miscommunication. I found that in a team, everyone must respect each other, share responsibilities, and cooperate. I also found that this can be important when achieving common objectives and considering other people's viewpoints.

Problem-Solving and Analytical Thinking

By studying the employee turnover at FreshCart, I was able to further develop my analytical and problem-solving abilities. I looked at issues such as unsuitable shifts, limited progression, and low salary. By looking at staff data and staff survey responses, I have seen the value of evidence in spotting issues within an organisation. I have also seen the benefit of looking at alternative options instead of basing decisions on individual intuition. It has enhanced my professional problem-solving by allowing me to use logic.

Leadership and Decision-Making

I have realised that good leadership is more than a matter of giving out roles. Developing staff, recognising success, and supporting staff can be ways of increasing motivation through the use of a case study. I also gained insight into the significance of fairness, responsible decision-making, accountability, and ethics whilst looking at the management issues facing FreshCart.

Challenges and Limitations

A problem for me was relating staff requirements and aims within appropriate solutions. I have found that my hands-on leadership experience is limited. I also require greater confidence in relation to decision-making and communicating my views.

Future Professional Development

I will aim to develop my skills via collaborative work, professional development, and practical experience. I will also continue to assess my own development and actively seek useful input. I would also like to enhance my confidence in communicating within the workplace and my leadership skills. This will enable me to be a more responsible, effective, and adaptable employee and make a contribution to the success of an organisation.
